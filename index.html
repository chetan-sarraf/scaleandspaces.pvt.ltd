Corporate Philosophy

Sensitizing Scale to Create Spaces that Inspire

Scale and Spaces Pvt. Ltd. was founded on the philosophy that architecture must harmonize structural rigor with human experience. Operating from our dual offices in Kathmandu and Birgunj, we deliver comprehensive solutions from conceptual sketches to turnkey construction.

Our multidisciplinary practice combines architectural planning, 3D photorealistic visualization, advanced civil engineering analysis (NBC 205:2024 / NBC 105:2020), and strict Bill of Quantities (BOQ) cost controls to prevent budget inflation.
